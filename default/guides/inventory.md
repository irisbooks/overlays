# Complete an inventory disposal

Record the disposal quantity, unit, settlement and fees. Confirm the acquisition history and annual opening basis, then configure `inventory.params.gain_account` in the Book. FIFO is the default allocation method. Preview and review the cost and gain/loss postings together before applying.

An uncovered sale or absent settlement is refused. Do not substitute cost for proceeds. Review the executable examples referenced by the task. Posting and inventory history remain host responsibilities.
